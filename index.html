Our University

BioMed Club is proud to be part of Navrachana University. A premier institution known for its interdisciplinary approach to education and excellence in research.

School of Science

The School of Science at Navrachana University focuses on in-depth scientific inquiries that delve into theory and push the frontiers of science to shape the future. The programs are designed to bridge the gap between the student’s aspirations and industry expectations.

The BioMed Club

The BioMed Club, established in 2018, wishes to reach all the curious minds and enriching them with better knowledge of Biomedical Sciences. The club strives to promote interactions among the students of various fields and discussion of innovative ideas together.
